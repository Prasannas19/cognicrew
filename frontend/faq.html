FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers about how CogniCrew works and what it does and does not guarantee.

What is CogniCrew? +
CogniCrew is an AI research assistant with four specialized agents: Web Researcher, Document Analyst, Fact Checker, and Report Writer. Ask a question or research a topic, and get a structured answer that shows exactly where every claim came from.
How does Document Research work? +
Upload your PDFs and ask a question. CogniCrew reads your documents page by page, retrieves the passages that actually discuss your question, and answers only from those passages. The web is never queried in document mode.
Can I research without uploading a PDF? +
Yes. Choose "Topic research" mode and enter any topic; the Web Researcher gathers findings from real web sources using Exa. No documents are required.
Can I upload multiple documents? +
Yes. Attach several PDFs to a single research run (such as lecture notes, textbook chapters, or slide decks) and select or remove any of them before launching. All attached documents are indexed together.
Does CogniCrew use web sources? +
Only in Topic Research when Web Search is enabled. In Document Research and AI Study Tools, your uploaded files are the only source, and the scope of every answer is verified and labelled.
How are sources displayed? +
Document sources show the document name, the PDF page number (and the printed book page number when confidently verified across adjacent pages), plus the retrieved excerpt. Web sources show the title, domain, and a real link. No source or page number is ever fabricated.
Can I ask follow-up questions? +
Yes. Every report has a follow-up chat that retrieves from the persisted document corpus. Follow-up answers provide citations, and continue working reliably even after a page refresh.
How does page-aware document retrieval work? +
PyMuPDF extracts text page by page on the server. Each page retains its document name, PDF page number, and content classification. Non-content pages like Tables of Contents, Indexes, and Bibliographies are classified and excluded from explanatory evidence.
What happens if an answer is not found? +
CogniCrew says so plainly: "I couldn't reliably locate explanatory evidence in the selected documents." It will never guess a page number or invent a source quotation.
Does CogniCrew guarantee factual accuracy? +
No. CogniCrew grounds answers in the passages it actually retrieved and lets you verify each claim against the shown source, but no AI system guarantees absolute accuracy. Always confirm important claims against the listed sources.
